# Dataset Metadata



##### Overview

This dataset contains socio-economic and well-being indicators for Switzerland over multiple years.

Each row represents a specific year for Switzerland, while the columns report different indicators related to income, employment, housing, education, safety, health, and life satisfaction.



The dataset includes 18 observations and 13 features.



##### Variables

* Reference area (string): country or geographical area name (constant: Switzerland).
* TIME\_PERIOD (integer): year of observation.
* Average annual gross earnings \[PPP converted] (float): average gross annual earnings, converted using Purchasing Power Parity.
* Difficulty making ends meet \[%] (float): percentage of population reporting difficulty in making ends meet.
* Employment rate \[%] (float): percentage of employed population.
* Feeling safe at night \[%] (float): percentage of population feeling safe when walking alone at night.
* Households and NPISHs net adjusted disposable income (float): net adjusted disposable income of households and non-profit institutions serving households.
* Housing affordability \[%] (float): percentage of households for which housing costs are considered affordable.
* Life expectancy at birth \[Years] (float): average life expectancy at birth, in years.
* Life satisfaction \[0–10 scale] (float): average self-reported life satisfaction on a scale from 0 to 10.
* Long-term unemployment rate \[%] (float) percentage of labour force unemployed for a long period.
* Social support \[%] (float): percentage of population reporting having social support.
* Student reading skills \[Points] (float): average student reading performance score.



##### Unit of Measure

* Percentages (%) are used for rate-based indicators.
* Years are used for life expectancy.
* Index points are used for student reading skills.
* Monetary values are expressed in PPP-converted units.
* Life satisfaction is measured on a 0–10 scale.
