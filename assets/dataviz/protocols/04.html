Protocol of the socioeconomic stability in Switzerland:

The visualization is based on 4 different visualization. Every visualization display a different index. We have CPI (consumer price index), GDP (gross domestic public), Unemployment rate, CHF - EU exchange.

Data used for the visualizations can be downloaded as a zip with 4 csv file in the previous page.

The initial data are 4 different dataset 1 for every visualization.
Unemployment EU vs CH: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/UNE_RT_M__custom_19512982/default/table
CHF/EU Exchange: https://www.investing.com/currencies/chf-eur-historical-data
IPC EU vs CH https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/prc_hicp_midx__custom_19512988/default/table
GDP EU vs CH https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sdg_08_10/default/table?lang=en

Preprocessing steps:

For every dataset we did a different preprocess according to the structure, in general:

1. Import of the datasets

2. Removed useless line and columns

3. Alligned Switzerland and European data.

The visualization allows users to understand the changes that happend during the crisies and visualize the Swiss stability
